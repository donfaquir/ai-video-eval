## Scoring Criteria

Rate the selling point coverage on a 5-level scale:

**Level 5:** All selling points are explicitly presented through both visual demonstration and text/speech. Each point receives clear, dedicated screen time with compelling evidence (e.g., close-up shots, data overlays, before/after comparisons).

**Level 4:** Most selling points (>=80%) are covered with clear visual or textual support. Minor points may lack dedicated demonstration but are still mentioned.

**Level 3:** About half of the selling points are covered. Some key points are presented visually while others are only mentioned briefly or implied.

**Level 2:** Only 1-2 selling points are addressed. Most key differentiators are missing from the video content entirely.

**Level 1:** No selling points are explicitly presented or demonstrated. The video fails to communicate any product advantages.

## Output Format

Return a JSON object with the following structure:
```json
{"level": <1-5>, "reasoning": "...", "evidence": [{"modality": "visual|audio|text", "timestamp": <float|null>, "detail": "..."}], "suggestion": "..."}
```

- `level`: Integer score from 1 to 5 based on the criteria above.
- `reasoning`: Brief explanation of why this level was assigned.
- `evidence`: List of specific observations supporting the score.
- `suggestion`: Actionable improvement advice (empty string if level >= 4).
