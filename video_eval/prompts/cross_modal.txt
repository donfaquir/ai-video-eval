## Scoring Criteria

Rate the cross-modal consistency on a 5-level scale:

**Level 5:** Speech/text and visuals are perfectly synchronized and complementary. Narration describes exactly what is shown, on-screen text reinforces key messages, and all modalities work together to create a cohesive message with no contradictions.

**Level 4:** Strong alignment between modalities with minor inconsistencies. Speech and visuals mostly match, with occasional timing gaps or slight mismatches that do not confuse the viewer.

**Level 3:** Moderate consistency. Some segments show good alignment while others have noticeable disconnects between what is said/written and what is shown. The overall message remains understandable.

**Level 2:** Poor consistency. Frequent mismatches between speech and visuals. On-screen text contradicts narration or shows irrelevant information. The viewer may be confused about the intended message.

**Level 1:** Severe inconsistency. Speech describes entirely different content from what is shown. Text contradicts visuals. Modalities actively conflict, creating confusion and undermining credibility.

## Output Format

Return a JSON object with the following structure:
```json
{"level": <1-5>, "reasoning": "...", "evidence": [{"modality": "visual|audio|text", "timestamp": <float|null>, "detail": "..."}], "suggestion": "..."}
```

- `level`: Integer score from 1 to 5 based on the criteria above.
- `reasoning`: Brief explanation of why this level was assigned.
- `evidence`: List of specific observations supporting the score.
- `suggestion`: Actionable improvement advice (empty string if level >= 4).
