## Scoring Criteria

Rate the audience match on a 5-level scale:

**Level 5:** Perfect audience alignment. Visual style, language register, scenarios, models/presenters, music, pacing, and messaging all precisely match the target demographic. The video feels tailor-made for its intended viewers and would strongly resonate with their values and preferences.

**Level 4:** Strong audience fit with minor gaps. Most elements align well with the target audience. One or two aspects (e.g., music choice, pacing) could be better optimized, but overall the video would appeal to its intended viewers.

**Level 3:** Moderate audience match. The video is generally appropriate for the target audience but uses generic approaches rather than audience-specific elements. Some scenes or messaging choices feel neutral rather than targeted.

**Level 2:** Weak audience match. Several elements feel misaligned with the target audience. Language may be too formal/informal, scenarios may be irrelevant to their lifestyle, or visual style may appeal to a different demographic.

**Level 1:** Complete mismatch. The video's style, tone, language, and content are entirely inappropriate for the target audience. It would likely alienate or confuse the intended viewers rather than engage them.

## Output Format

Return a JSON object with the following structure:
```json
{"level": <1-5>, "reasoning": "...", "evidence": [{"modality": "visual|audio|text", "timestamp": <float|null>, "detail": "..."}], "suggestion": "..."}
```

- `level`: Integer score from 1 to 5 based on the criteria above.
- `reasoning`: Brief explanation of why this level was assigned.
- `evidence`: List of specific observations supporting the score.
- `suggestion`: Actionable improvement advice (empty string if level >= 4).
